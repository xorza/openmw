# Picture determinism, `rtx <flavour> repeat`. Newest first.
#
# The scene columns are exact at every reading here, so what a line records is the picture; since
# 2026-09-15 the picture is the gate as well. A count of differing pictures is how early one
# divergence landed and not how much went wrong: `AGENTS.md` says why the measured exposure carries
# one frame's difference into every frame after it, and why a count therefore scales with the walk it
# was taken over.


- The picture a walk draws does not repeat between two runs, and what makes it differ is not known.
  `.notes/repeatable.txt` holds the readings: the rate moves between sessions by more than any
  change measured against it, and three readings an hour apart over one binary ran from ten pairs of
  ten identical to none of four. The scene columns repeat on every pair, which is why this is a
  report and not a gate.
