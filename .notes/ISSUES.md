# Open issues
- A run with Ray Reconstruction on does not repeat itself, in the debug and the release flavour alike. `bench --views=balmora-mages-guild,balmora-storm-night --warmup=0 --seconds=2 --exposure=1 --hashes=a` and then `--against=a` on one binary differs in about one run of three (debug 5 of 12, release 2 of 8), with every scene and constants column identical and every frame before the flip byte-identical. The flip is one pixel one step of 255 apart, at a frame the network picks — the guild's frame 7 and the storm's frame 2 under preset d, the guild's frame 3 and the storm's frames 2 and 52 under preset e — and it is the same alternate picture every time it happens. Ray Reconstruction's history then spreads it: 3567 pixels a frame later, 4% of the frame by frame 20, and under a measured exposure a brightness shift over the frame, which is the storm's 19 of 255 on 11% in `shot --views=all --map` and `one-cell-walk`'s 288 of 1200 frames from frame 913 in release. It happens with the device drained after every frame, so it is not an overlap between frames, and the trace itself repeats: `--upscale=off` at 1920x1080, at 1280x720 and at 1280x720 with `--jitter=true` was identical on 40 of 40 pairs in debug and on 6 of 6 in release, `repeat --pairs=2` in release included. `shot --against` and `bench --hashes --against` at their defaults report these flips as moved pictures, while `repeat` runs `--upscale=off` and never sees one. Measured 2026-09-19 on 610.57.04 with the shipped NGX library.
