# Open issues

- A slot that stands in (a file that does not read, a texture past the side limit, or one the device has no room for) draws `describeStandIn`'s mid-grey `Bc1RgbaSrgb` block, about 0.23 in every channel, and every shader that reads the slot takes that grey as the texture's content. A normal map, on an object or a ground layer, gives a tangent-space normal of about (-0.54, -0.54, -0.54), which points into the surface. A specular map makes the material "not delit", with metalness and roughness of 0.23. A dark map multiplies the surface by 0.23. An emissive map makes the surface glow, and an environment sheet adds a grey sheet. An authored ground layer, or a distant chunk's gloss, gives the ground a lobe. A distant chunk's composite, the first texture to give way when the device runs short of memory, turns the whole chunk grey.
